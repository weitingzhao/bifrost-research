## Behavior

- Screen coverage counts the latest usable score in its existing seven-day
  window. NULL, NaN and infinity are excluded; numerical zero remains readable.
  Filtering happens after latest-row selection, so an older score cannot mask
  a newer withheld reading. Unbanded categorical readings remain readable.
- IV Rank and IV Percentile exhibits explain insufficient history, unknown
  history depth, and missing scores despite sufficient history separately.
  No row remains missing data. The engine's 126-session gate is unchanged and
  its constant is reused by the explanation.
- Symbol Volatility uses Research's rank and percentile instead of recomputing
  them from a short VRP history. It shows the existing caveat beside withheld
  scores. The illustrative rank path is withheld when rank is unavailable;
  current IV30 remains visible; mature historical ranges are labelled history low/high.
